Setoo

UI Concept and Product Design for insurance marketing dashboard.

Insuretech

Client

Setoo is a provider of insurance marketing tools. Setoo's insurance and protection as a service platform empowers e-businesses to turn insurance into products consumers love.

My role

UI concept design.

The challenge

To design a clean looking web based dashboard. This dashboard would be used to manage and create insurance offers, analyse sales and get insights. The design needed to "pop" and look good at investor presentations, as raising capital was the main goal for Setoo at that time.

Result

Setoo secured a $9.3 million Series A funding round after I designed their insurance marketing dashboard.
